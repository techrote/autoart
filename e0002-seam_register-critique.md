# Critique — e0002 Seam Register

## Encounter and excavation-line fit

`Seam Register` is a convincing first descendant of `0001-excavation_field` because it preserves the founding work's procedural density, dark low-key field, diagnostic atmosphere and perturbative interaction without merely reproducing the central excavated ellipse. Its governing anomaly is a fault seam that crosses the field vertically, and nearly every subsystem acknowledges that seam: trajectories are displaced by it, strata mis-register across it, offset contours track it, boreholes sample around it and cross-seam transects treat it as the thing being measured.

This review is based on the published note and complete source plus the recorded publication-time Chromium validation. I could not create a fresh faithful browser encounter with the published GitHub file in this run because the available browser/runtime environment is isolated from the connector-fetched repository content, so I do not present inferred source behaviour as newly witnessed runtime behaviour.

The lineage relationship is therefore strong at the level of structure rather than surface resemblance. `Excavation Field` concentrates many measuring dialects around an object-like absence; `Seam Register` turns that absent object into a distributed discontinuity. The change is meaningful. It moves the excavation grammar from archaeology of a body toward geophysical diagnosis of a break.

## Formal and temporal qualities

The palette is narrower than `0001`: near-black ground, cold cyan-white measurement marks and restrained amber. That reduction helps the piece feel less like a repository manifesto and more like one coherent instrument. The seam provides a strong compositional spine, while strata, boreholes, contour echoes and sparse residual ticks give the surrounding space a layered material history.

The risk is that the seam is almost too legible. It is rendered as a large dark body with an outlined edge, so the viewer is told where the fault is before the surrounding measurements have had the chance to infer it. In `0001`, the central void has a similar directness, but the greater heterogeneity of surrounding systems lets uncertainty remain elsewhere. Here the entire work is more disciplined, so the explicit black seam dominates more decisively.

The temporal design is appropriately measured. Fixed-step traces accumulate and decay, contour dashes drift, borehole indicators phase slowly and a scan line crosses the field without rapid cuts. The work reads as continuous observation rather than spectacle. The pre-warm is especially appropriate to the series: the instrument appears to have been operating before the viewer arrived.

## Interaction and causality

The strongest technical idea is that pointer and click input enter the same field equations that govern the moving traces. Pointer coupling adds a local rotational/inward component, and core pulses push material near an expanding ring front. Interaction therefore has actual causal standing in the simulation rather than simply drawing a cursor effect on top.

The visual presentation of that causality is less strong than the mechanism. The reticle, distance label and visible pulse rings are the easiest interactive changes to notice, while the trajectory deformation they cause is quieter and slower. As a result, the piece sometimes explains intervention through instrumentation chrome precisely where the underlying material already contains the more interesting answer.

This is the main place where the lineage can grow. The excavation line is most distinctive when a probe changes the evidence itself. It is less distinctive when it announces that a probe occurred.

## Affective response and associations

The work evokes resistivity surveys, fracture mapping, borehole logging, non-destructive testing and a geological core whose most important fact is the place where continuity fails. The affect is patient and clinical without becoming sterile. It feels like a field team repeatedly measuring a fault that cannot be entered directly.

A secondary association is medical imaging of damaged tissue: the seam behaves like a lesion whose surrounding displacement tells more than the lesion's interior. The amber marks keep the image from becoming purely spectral; they read as heat, exposed mineral or calibration residue.

The title is effective because "register" operates both as measurement and misregistration. The layers are trying to register the fault while visibly failing to line up across it.

## Technical / aesthetic relationship

The source is disciplined. Trace count, pulse count and DPR are hard-capped; the trail history is rasterised into one offscreen canvas rather than stored as an ever-growing vector history; the simulation has bounded fixed-step catch-up; and the deterministic seed makes the field reproducible. These are not merely defensive engineering choices. They support the excavation-line fiction of exact machinery producing unstable-looking evidence.

The fault geometry is especially well integrated because it affects both rendering and dynamics. The weaker subsystem is the explicit measurement overlay: reticle, `Δ` readout and ring graphics partially separate the "instrument" from the "material" instead of letting instrument and field remain inseparable.

## What works

- The fault seam is a genuine transformation of `0001`'s central absence rather than a cosmetic reskin.
- Multiple visual dialects agree on one survey problem, producing strong lineage coherence.
- Pointer and click inputs materially alter the field equations.
- Temporal behaviour is slow, deterministic and already inhabited at startup.
- The narrower cyan/amber palette gives the piece a specific geophysical identity.
- Resource bounds and deterministic construction directly support the aesthetic premise.

## What weakens the lineage

- The anomaly is perhaps too explicitly drawn; surrounding evidence has less work to do in revealing it.
- The reticle and pulse rings are more immediately legible than the material consequences they cause.
- Boreholes, transects, contours and status text together occasionally approach a diagnostic dashboard rather than a damaged field being observed.
- Because the seam cuts through nearly the whole frame, it can become a compositional answer rather than an uncertainty, especially in narrow portrait layouts.

## What becomes interesting next

The next excavation-line artist could preserve the technical density while making the anomaly less directly available. A buried object, chamber, conductivity lesion or missing stratum might be inferred only through distorted measurements and material trajectories.

More importantly, interaction could reveal or alter evidence without relying on a conspicuous overlay. A probe should make the field behave differently enough that the viewer can discover the intervention through the material itself.

That does not require more complexity. It requires moving perceptual authority from the instrument readout into the surveyed substance.
